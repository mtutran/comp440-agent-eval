# Rubric: How well did the support agent handle this customer?

## Specific
Does the customer leave knowing where things stand and what the remedy is?

- 1: No answer, only reassurance, or the agent misreads the problem.
- 2: A status with no remedy, or a remedy based on a vague cause, a guess or a statement that contradicts itself.
- 3: Correct status and a remedy, but a detail the customer needed is missing, so they still have to guess something important.
- 4: Correct status and a concrete remedy, with one minor gap in what the customer needed.
- 5: Status and remedy are complete for what this customer needed. The cause is given if the customer asked why or it affects what they should do. If the customer only asked a question, a full and correct answer scores 5.

## Action
Did the agent do the work on the call, or leave it to the customer?

- 1: Nothing was done; the customer is told to wait, call back or handle it themselves.
- 2: The agent only looked things up or logged an escalation when it could have done more.
- 3: The agent started the fix but left a step it could have done.
- 4: The agent completed the main action, and the customer has only physical steps left. Or if the customer only needed information, there was nothing else the agent could do for them on the call and the agent gave correct information.
- 5: The agent completed the main action and confirmed it worked on the call, or removed the customer's remaining steps. A missing confirmation number alone does not lower the score.

## Next Steps
Does the customer know what happens after the call?

- 1: No next step.
- 2: A next step with no timeframe.
- 3: A next step with a timeframe, but it's unclear who acts or how the customer will hear about it.
- 4: A next step with a timeframe and how the customer will be notified or can track it, but no fallback if it doesn't happen.
- 5: A next step with a timeframe, how the customer will be notified or can track it, and what to do if it doesn't happen. If nothing is pending after the call and the agent's answer was not wrong or contradictory.
